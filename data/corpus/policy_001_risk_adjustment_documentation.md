> SYNTHETIC DATA. This policy was written for a technical take-home exercise. It does not represent any real payer's policy and must not be used for real submissions.

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the minimum documentation standards for a diagnosis to be submitted for risk adjustment under Example Health Plan's Medicare Advantage contracts.

## 2. Acceptable Encounter Types
2.1 A diagnosis may be submitted only when it is documented during a face-to-face encounter or an interactive audio-and-video telehealth encounter that meets the requirements of Policy RA-110.
2.2 Diagnoses documented only on the following are not acceptable sources: laboratory-only or radiology-only reports, durable medical equipment orders, prescription refills without an encounter, problem lists that are not addressed during the encounter, and superbills.

## 3. Acceptable Provider Types
Acceptable providers are physicians (MD, DO), nurse practitioners (NP), physician assistants (PA), and clinical nurse specialists (CNS). Diagnoses documented solely by registered nurses, medical assistants, dietitians, or pharmacists are not acceptable for submission.

## 4. MEAT Criteria
4.1 Each chronic condition must be supported by at least one element of MEAT in the encounter note: Monitor (signs, symptoms, disease progression or regression), Evaluate (test results, medication effectiveness, response to treatment), Assess or Address (ordering tests, discussion, review of records, counseling), or Treat (medications, therapies, other modalities).
4.2 A diagnosis listed only in the past medical history, without any MEAT element in the current encounter, does not meet the standard.
4.3 Conditions described as resolved, historical, or "history of" must be coded with the appropriate personal history code (categories Z85 through Z87) and are not submitted as active conditions.

## 5. Signature and Dating
5.1 The note must contain the date of service and a legible provider signature with credentials. Electronic signatures must include a date and time stamp.
5.2 Unsigned notes, and notes signed by a provider other than the rendering provider, are excluded.

## 6. Chronic Condition Recapture
Chronic conditions (for example diabetes, chronic kidney disease, heart failure, and COPD) must be documented and supported with MEAT criteria at least once per calendar year to be submitted for that payment year. Documentation from a prior year does not carry forward.

## 7. Coder Review
All diagnoses extracted from unstructured notes, whether by a human coder or by an automated tool, must be reviewed and confirmed by a certified risk adjustment coder before submission. Automated extraction output is considered a recommendation, not a submission.
